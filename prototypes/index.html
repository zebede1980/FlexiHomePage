FlexiHome — design directions

Three takes on the same bookmarks. Move the mouse around, click the background, hover cards, rows and pinned icons, and rest on a link for the preview. Visual only: drag-and-drop, menus and editing aren't wired up here.

A · Aurora

  • Drifting colour fields that lean toward the cursor and flare with speed
  • Glass cards whose borders light up from the cursor, even across neighbours
  • One highlight that glides between rows, tinted per site
  • Magnetic pinned icons, brand-colour glow

B · Constellation

  • Particle network that scatters from the cursor and wires itself to it; parallax depth
  • HUD panels: 3D tilt, corner brackets, title "decode" effect
  • Rows scan and reveal the domain; orbiting rings on pinned icons
  • Terminal-style preview with typewriter text

C · Dot Field

  • Light, warm, editorial. A dot grid that breathes and swells under a lens around the cursor
  • macOS-style magnifying dock for pinned links
  • Focus mode: the card you're in lifts, the others step back
  • Rows wash with the site's colour and unfold the domain; polaroid preview

Click anywhere on the background in each one for a ripple. All three pause animation when the tab is hidden and respect "reduce motion".
